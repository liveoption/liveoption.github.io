01

Observe the market at the decision time

LiveOption assembles the configured market and event context from QFinZero services for the agent.

02

Turn a decision into simulated execution

Agent proposals are checked against task rules and passed to the paper broker. Orders, fills and portfolio states remain distinct records.

03

Replay and evaluate the outcome

Recorded decisions and execution traces support the benchmark’s return, risk and behaviour analysis.

LiveOption defines the option-trading benchmark and agent workflow. QFinZero runs separately and supplies the financial services; it is an open-source project under the MIT License.
